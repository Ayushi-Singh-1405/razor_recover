================================================================
  6. Takeaways for Gate B
================================================================

  - Nemotron's reasoning quality is strong (no failure_reason-only
    decisions; correct withholding of all non-recoverable events).
  - Its cost is conservatism: 3 missed recoverable events, plus one
    format failure that discarded good reasoning.
  - On identical events, the auto-router out-earned nemotron by
    Rs 27.4K — driven by format robustness and less conservative
    escalation, not by better per-attempt precision (both 0 bad
    interventions; router 11/11 vs nemotron 8/8 on recoverable
    attempts).
  - Full-run economics (662 events, openrouter/free) remain the
    authoritative comparison: day3_experiment_result.txt.
