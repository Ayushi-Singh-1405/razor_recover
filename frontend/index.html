Turn failed payments into recovered revenue.

Failed payments create recoverable revenue loss. Repechage identifies at-risk payment events, evaluates recovery opportunities, chooses an appropriate intervention, and records the result.

How Repechage works

01 Detect→ 02 Diagnose→ 03 Decide→ 04 Policy Gate→ 05 Act→ 06 Audit

Identify payment failures and at-risk events.

02

03

Decide

The recovery agent evaluates the available context.

A deterministic safety layer validates the proposed action.

05

Recover the payment or escalate to the merchant.

06

Audit

Record the decision and outcome.

AI decides. Policy controls.

The recovery agent can recommend an action, but execution is constrained by the deterministic Policy Gate. Cases that require human judgment can be escalated to the merchant.
